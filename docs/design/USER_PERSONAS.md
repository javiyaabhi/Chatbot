# User Personas

## Primary persona: College learner
A student studying DBMS or programming who wants concise explanations, examples, and follow-up practice in a preferred language.

## Secondary persona: Beginner self-learner
A learner with limited subject confidence who needs approachable vocabulary and step-by-step explanations.

## Tertiary persona: Exam preparer
A student who needs structured review, quizzes, and concise revision-oriented answers.
