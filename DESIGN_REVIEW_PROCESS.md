# Design Review Process

1. Discovery by Design Researcher, Brand Director, and UX Architect.
2. Three genuine concept directions evaluated for product suitability and accessibility.
3. CDO approval of one direction.
4. UX flow and wireframe review.
5. Design Systems Engineer token and component specification.
6. UI high-fidelity specification.
7. Motion specification and reduced-motion treatment.
8. Design Engineer implementation with Frontend Lead.
9. Accessibility audit and Visual QA browser review.
10. CDO and CTO final approval.
