# EduVaani Creative Direction

## Direction: Quiet Academic

A trusted learning environment built from deep green, warm white, readable type, restrained motion, and clear learning hierarchy.

## Intent
- Make the next learning action obvious.
- Preserve reading comfort for long AI explanations.
- Use language selection as a first-class, visible control.
- Keep Gujarati and Hindi text naturally readable.
- Separate teaching content, navigation, and account actions.
